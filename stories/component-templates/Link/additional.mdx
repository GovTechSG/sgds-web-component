> **SSR apps (Next.js, Nuxt, Angular SSR, etc.):** To avoid hydration mismatches, add `disabled` directly on the `<a>` and remove `href`:
>
> ```html
> <sgds-link disabled><a disabled>Disabled link</a></sgds-link>
> ```
