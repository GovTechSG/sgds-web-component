## Fluid

By default, the Mainnav content is contained within a max-width container. Use the `fluid` prop to remove this constraint and allow the content to stretch to the full screen width.

- Use `fluid` mainnav when you need the navigation bar to span the full viewport width.

<Canvas of={MainnavStories.Fluid}>
  <Story of={MainnavStories.Fluid} />
</Canvas>

## Tone

The `tone` prop controls the visual theme of the navigation bar. All non-default tones render text and icons in fixed-light (white) and are typically paired with `fluid`.

### Brand

Uses the primary surface colour as the background.

<Canvas of={MainnavStories.ToneBrand}>
  <Story of={MainnavStories.ToneBrand} />
</Canvas>

### Gradient 1

Cyan to Purple gradient background.
